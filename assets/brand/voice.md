# Voice

How LoLMK talks. The website should sound like the community: confident, welcoming, in-on-the-joke, but never cringe. We're not Riot, not a corporate esports org, not a Discord meme — we're somewhere in the middle.

## Tone in one line

**Confident insider, helpful friend.** We've been on KR for years and we know what we're talking about. We're glad you're here, but we're not going to baby you.

## Voice attributes

### We are

- **Direct** — Say the thing. "Make a KR account" not "Embark on your journey to the Korean server."
- **Practical** — Useful info first. Vibes second.
- **Confident** — We know KR. We don't hedge unnecessarily.
- **Welcoming** — New players, tourists, expats, returnees — all welcome. Never gatekeep.
- **Bilingual-aware** — KR-specific terms are fine. Korean phrases occasionally, with translations.
- **Esports-literate** — We assume you know what LCK is. We don't assume you know what 정글차이 is — that's a fun aside.

### We are not

- **Corporate** — Don't say "synergize," "leverage," "passionate community," "elevate your gameplay."
- **Try-hard hype** — No "EPIC," "INSANE," "GAME-CHANGING," "the ULTIMATE community," excessive caps or exclamation marks.
- **Overly casual** — We're not a Discord meme channel. No "lol fr fr," no "bussin," no "skibidi" anything on the public site.
- **Apologetic** — Don't pad with "if you'd like to" or "we hope you enjoy." Just say it.
- **Generic gaming brand** — No "for gamers, by gamers." No "join the squad." No "level up your experience."

## Vocabulary

### Use

- KR / KR server (always abbreviated, capital K-R)
- LoL Park (the venue)
- LCK (always capitalized, no "the")
- in-house, scrim, tournament, bracket
- meetup (one word)
- PC bang (or 피시방 in Korean contexts, italicized first use)
- IGN (in-game name)
- duo, flex, ranked, normals — all lowercase
- KakaoTalk (one word, capitalized correctly) — "Kakao" alone is acceptable in body copy after first reference

### Avoid

- "Gamers" as a noun ("gamers welcome" — no)
- "The community" — say "LoLMK" or "we"
- "Solo queue" without context — most KR-server people say "solo q" or just "ranked"
- "Boba" or unrelated Gen Z markers — not the vibe
- "Korean server" — use "KR" or "KR server"
- "League" alone when "LoL" or "LoL on KR" is clearer

## Formality scale

Different pages have different default formality. Calibrate:

- **Hero / landing copy**: confident-casual. Short sentences. ("English-speaking League on the Korean server. Find your players.")
- **How-to articles**: instructional, second-person. Direct steps. ("Open your account settings. Click the language dropdown.")
- **Event descriptions**: factual + a sentence of personality. ("Q2 2026 Tournament. 16 teams, double elim, runs across two weekends. Last quarter's champs are back to defend — let's see if anyone has the wave management to dethrone them.")
- **Error pages**: short, light, on-brand. ("404 — wrong lane. Let's get you back to base.")
- **Tournament rules / formal docs**: dry and clear. No personality. People are reading this to win.

## Headline patterns that work

- Stating the fact, confidently. "The biggest English-speaking LoL community on KR."
- The benefit + a verb. "Find duos. Run scrims. Watch LCK with people who care."
- The question that's already answered. "Looking for English-speaking ranked? You're here."
- Short + punchy + accent on the last word. "Built for the **KR grind**."

## Headlines to avoid

- "Welcome to LoLMK!" — generic
- "Where champions unite" — every esports site has written this
- "The ultimate destination for League fans in Korea" — meaningless
- "Join us today!" — weak CTA, not a headline

## CTA copy

CTAs should be specific verbs, not generic invitations.

### Use

- Join the Discord
- Message us on Kakao
- See upcoming events
- Read the KR account guide
- Register for Q2 Tournament
- Browse members

### Avoid

- Click here
- Learn more (sometimes okay, but try harder)
- Get started
- Find out more
- Sign up now

## Microcopy examples

**Empty states**

- Members page (no filter results): "No one matches that filter. Try fewer filters or browse everyone."
- Past tournaments (none yet): "Q1 2026 was the first tournament. Past tournaments will live here once we have some past tournaments to show."
- Shop (empty section): "Nothing here yet. Drops happen quarterly — keep an eye on Discord."

**Loading states**

- Just "Loading…" is fine. Don't try to be clever every time.

**Error states**

- 404: "Wrong lane. This page doesn't exist."
- 500: "Something broke on our end. Refresh, or hop in Discord and let us know."

## How to use this doc

When writing any visible copy on the site (headlines, body, buttons, error messages), check it against this voice:

1. Does it sound like a confident insider, or like a corporate brand?
2. Are we using the actual vocabulary the community uses?
3. Are CTAs specific verbs?
4. Are we adding personality where it fits, or trying to be funny where we shouldn't?
5. Would an LCK fan reading this think "yeah, these people get it"?

If a piece of copy fails any of these, rewrite it.

## Open questions

- How much Korean to include in headers? Leaning: not much. English-first site, Korean used for specific terms only (PC bang, names of places).
- Do we add a "newsletter voice" if we ever do email? Probably yes — slightly more casual than the site, slightly less than Discord.
